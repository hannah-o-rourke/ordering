We cannot solve pressures on hospital beds by simply transferring that pressure onto families. If somebody is going home, care must be waiting for them, the family must know what is happening and unpaid carers must be properly supported. That isn't separate from protecting Withybush Hospital; it's part of protecting Withybush Hospital. Because the answer to pressure on hospital beds cannot simply be fewer hospital beds. We need to prevent unnecessary admissions to make sure they're ready to leave and can do safely.

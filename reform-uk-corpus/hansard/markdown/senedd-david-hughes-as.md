## 2026-10-20 — 4. Statement by the First Minister: 60th Anniversary of the Aberfan Disaster

*Speech* · [https://record.senedd.wales/Plenary/16317#C779996](https://record.senedd.wales/Plenary/16317#C779996)

Like Lis, I was brought up in Aberfan and had many friends who were there that day. So, today, we pause with heavy hearts to remember the lives lost in Aberfan's disaster. Sixty years on, the pain of that terrible day remains deeply felt by families, friends and the community whose lives were forever changed. We remember the children whose lives were taken far too soon, the adults who lost their lives and all those who witnessed such unimaginable grief. Aberfan was not simply a tragedy in history; it was a community's heartbreak and a reminder of how precious every life is.

Today we honour those who were lost, remember all those who carry on and hold the families and people of Aberfan in our thoughts. May the memories never fade, may the compassion, courage and strength shown in the aftermath continue to inspire us. We will remember them, we will grieve and we will never forget. That's all I ask of this place: that we never forget. Thank you.

## Political context as of 6 November 2026 (needed to read the polls)

### Takeaway
2026 is the year the Reform UK lead collapsed and Labour recovered under a new Prime Minister: Keir Starmer announced his resignation on 22 June 2026 after Labour's May election losses; Andy Burnham (returned to the Commons at the 18 June Makerfield by-election) became Labour leader and Prime Minister on 17–20 July 2026, and a "Burnham bounce" took Labour from ~18–19% to ~28–30% by October, with Reform falling from ~29–31% to ~20–25% and the Conservatives (still led by Kemi Badenoch) recovering to ~20–24%.

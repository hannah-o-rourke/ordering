Research method note. All figures below were gathered on 6 Nov 2026 via WebSearch result snippets (c. 65 searches). ## KQ1. Who is Prime Minister and who leads each party as of 6 Nov 2026; leadership changes during 2026

### Takeaway
2026 saw a change of Prime Minister without a general election: Keir Starmer announced his resignation on 22 June 2026 after Labour's historic defeat in the 7 May local/devolved elections and Andy Burnham's return to the Commons at the 18 June Makerfield by-election; Burnham was confirmed Labour leader unopposed on 17 July and became PM on 20 July 2026. Other UK party leaders were unchanged (Badenoch, Farage, Davey, Polanski, Swinney), but Wales changed First Minister (Plaid's Rhun ap Iorwerth replaced Labour's Eluned Morgan on 12 May) and Welsh Labour changed leader (Ken Skates).

## KQ2. Leader net ratings at the start of 2026 and now; month-by-month trend by pollster

### Takeaway
Starmer began 2026 at record lows (YouGov net favourability -57 in January, Ipsos net satisfaction -62) and left office at -45 (YouGov, 14–15 Jun) / -59 (Ipsos, 25–30 Jun, the worst final rating for any PM in Ipsos data since 1979). Burnham entered at +3 (YouGov, late Jul) and peaked at +13 (13–14 Aug; best for any PM on YouGov since May 2020), sliding to +4 by 15–16 Oct as unfavourables rose to 42%; Opinium has him +16 (29 Jul) → +7 (28 Oct); Ipsos satisfaction 35% vs 45% (Oct). Badenoch improved through the year (YouGov -26 Jan → -11 Jul best-ever → -15 Oct). Farage collapsed (YouGov -37 Jan → -46 Jul, joint-worst in 10 years → -45 Sep, 69% unfavourable, record → -42 Oct). Polanski fell from -14 (Mar) to -37 (Oct, record low). Davey drifted from -9 to -16 (worst since the 2024 election).

### Inferences
- Across pollsters, Burnham's honeymoon peaked in mid-August and has roughly halved by late October, but he remains the only net-positive UK leader on every series (YouGov +4, Opinium +7, More in Common +8/+9, Ipsos favourability +8) — except Ipsos's harder "satisfied with job as PM" measure (-10), which has a large don't-know share.
- Farage's deterioration is the other big story: he is now at or near record lows on YouGov (-42 to -46), Ipsos (-31) and More in Common (-27/-28), with Reform's Scottish rating at -56.
- Badenoch's recovery is real but modest (YouGov -26 → -15; Opinium/More in Common near zero), and Edelman notes only about a third of people who like her would vote Conservative.

## KQ3. Government approval / right direction–wrong track, 2026

### Takeaway

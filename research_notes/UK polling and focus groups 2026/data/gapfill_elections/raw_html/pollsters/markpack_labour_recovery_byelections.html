As for the Parliamentary by-election:

Big thanks to Patrick Stillman for waving the flag for us in this by-election, and to everyone who supported him. If my canvassing was anything to go by, there were a lot of people who were sure to be voting Labour in order to stop Zack Polanski, but will be open to voting for us in the local elections – and Patrick’s campaign impressed them, making it easier to win them over next time.

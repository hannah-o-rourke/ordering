Technical Note
This month’s Issues Index has been conducted over the phone, a methodology
change which has been in place since the April 2020 wave. Mode effects should be
kept in mind when comparing the data since April 2020 with previous months.

Ipsos interviewed a representative sample of 1,004 adults aged 18+ across Great
Britain. Interviews were conducted by telephone between 7 and 13 January 2026. The
data is weighted to match the profile of the population.

Where percentages do not sum to 100, this may be due to computer rounding, the
exclusion of “don’t know” categories, or multiple answers. An asterisk (*) denotes any
value of less than half a per cent, but more than zero.

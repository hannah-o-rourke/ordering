## KQ1. Leader ratings and government approval, month by month Jan–Nov 2026, from primary tables; resolve the Starmer -36 vs -45 conflict

### Takeaway
The complete 2026 series now rests on primary tables rather than snippets: 210 YouGov favourability waves, 44 More in Common weekly tables, 31 Opinium fortnightly tables, 6 Ipsos Political Monitor table PDFs, 4 Deltapoll PDFs and 7 YouGov political-tracker PDFs. The -36/-45 conflict is resolved: both are YouGov figures for Starmer but for different waves — -45 (24% fav / 69% unfav, n=2,099) is the 14–15 June wave, the last before his 22 June resignation announcement (repeated at -45 on 21–22 June), while -36 (28/64, n=2,104) is the 12–13 July wave, taken while he was still caretaker PM (Burnham appointed 20 July) and therefore the "final rating as prime minister" quoted in YouGov's October article. Government approval tracked separately from Burnham's personal ratings: YouGov's tracker question moved only from -52 (Jan) / -55 (Feb) to -39 (25–27 Jul), -33 (22–24 Aug) and -34 (19–21 Sep); Ipsos government satisfaction went from -66 (Jan) and -66 (May) to -52 (Jul/Aug) and -43 (Oct, best since 2022).
